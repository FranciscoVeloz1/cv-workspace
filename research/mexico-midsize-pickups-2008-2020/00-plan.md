# Research Plan: Cheap-but-reliable midsize pickups in Mexico, 2008–2020

## Objective
Identify, for each model year 2008 through 2020, the top five midsize pickup trucks available in Mexico that best fit a **cheap but reliable** buyer (low purchase/used price, durable powertrains, parts availability, reasonable running costs). Support a used-truck purchase decision in Mexico in 2026 with per-year rankings, engine/horsepower/transmission specs, current Mexican resale prices, and known advantages and problems.

## Scope
**In scope:**
- Midsize (compact / mediana) pickups sold new in Mexico, model years 2008–2020
- Top 5 per year ranked primarily by cheap-but-reliable value, with sales/popularity as supporting context
- Technical specs: engines, horsepower, transmissions (and notable drivetrain variants)
- Reliability reputation and common mechanical/electrical problems
- Mexican used-market / resale prices (2026 peso figures where available)
- Advantages and problems per leading nameplate

**Out of scope:**
- Full-size / heavy-duty pickups (e.g. Ford F-150, Chevrolet Silverado 1500/2500, RAM 1500) except as a boundary for what “midsize” excludes
- Model years before 2008 or after 2020
- New-vehicle MSRP-only shopping without used-market context
- Insurance quotes, financing products, and theft-rate deep dives beyond what reliability/resale sources mention
- Opinion rankings that cannot be tied to sales, specs, prices, or documented reliability claims

## Source policy
Prioritize official/primary sources. Reputable secondary sources allowed for corroboration
and context. Exclude unattributed or unverifiable sources. No invented data or citations
anywhere in this project.

Preferred types: AMIA / INEGI / manufacturer Mexican catalogs and press kits; Autocosmos, Motorpasión México, El Economista, Expansión, official brand México sites; used-price listings and market reports (Mercado Libre aggregates, Autocosmos usados, Kelley/Guía de precios if published); recall/TSB or independent reliability write-ups.
