# Research Plan: Globally best midsize pickups sold in Mexico, 2008–2020

## Objective
Identify, for each model year 2008 through 2020, the top five **globally class-leading** midsize pickup trucks that were **sold new in Mexico**. “Best” means documented international standing (global sales among midsize/one-tonne trucks, independent crash ratings, dependability studies, and established press as class leaders)—not Mexican volume leadership and not cheapest-used. Support a 2026 used-truck decision in Mexico with per-year rankings, Mexican-market engines/horsepower/transmissions, current Mexican resale prices, and documented advantages and problems.

## Scope
**In scope:**
- Midsize (global one-tonne / “pick up mediana”) trucks sold new in Mexico, model years 2008–2020
- Ranking by **global class standing among those Mexican-market trucks**
- Technical specs for Mexican-market engines, horsepower, transmissions
- Reliability, crash-test, and known problems
- Mexican used/resale prices (2026 peso figures where available)
- Advantages and problems per leading nameplate

**Out of scope:**
- Full-size / heavy-duty pickups (Ford F-150/Lobo, Chevrolet Silverado/Cheyenne, RAM 1500, Tundra) even if they lead *global pickup volume*
- Nameplates that were globally acclaimed but **not sold new in Mexico in 2008–2020** (e.g. Isuzu D-Max, Mazda BT-50, Mercedes-Benz X-Class) except as a boundary in the counter-narrative
- Compact FWD work trucks (Chevrolet Tornado, RAM 700, Volkswagen Saveiro, Renault Oroch) as ranking candidates
- Model years before 2008 or after 2020
- Opinion lists that cannot be tied to sales, crash tests, specs, prices, or documented reliability claims

## Source policy
Prioritize official/primary sources. Reputable secondary sources allowed for corroboration
and context. Exclude unattributed or unverifiable sources. No invented data or citations
anywhere in this project.

Preferred types: manufacturer Mexican catalogs and press kits; ANCAP / Latin NCAP; AMIA/INEGI via established press; Autocosmos, Motorpasión México; global volume tallies from established industry sources; J.D. Power / established US press for Tacoma; Guía Autométrica via Autocosmos.
