# q02: For each calendar/model year 2008–2020, which midsize pickups led Mexican sales or registrations (up to a top 5)?

**Status:** researched — pending validation
**Source types to prioritize:** AMIA monthly/annual sales, INEGI, El Economista/Expansión roundups

## Findings

Public, model-level **annual top-5 lists for every year 2008–2020 do not exist in one official table**. INEGI RAIAVL is the underlying register (AMIA/AMDA republish it); monthly PDFs fetched for 2018–2020 are brand totals, not model ranks [S1]. What follows is assembled from AMIA/AMDA figures as republished by Autocosmos, T21, TyT, Nissan, El Financiero and Transporte.mx. Where sources conflict, both figures are kept.

### Segment backdrop

Transporte.mx, citing AMDA, reports pickup sales peaked at **217,667** in 2007, then **154,724 (2009)**, **149,352 (2010)**, **138,713 (2011)**, recovering to about **180,000** in the year of that article (context: 2015’s 180,073 pickups, 12% of new-vehicle sales) [S2]. TyT, citing AMIA, reports **211,746** pickups in 2016 (+17.6% vs 2015) [S3]. T21, citing INEGI, reports **184,439** pickups in 2018 and **193,332** in 2019 [S4]. Autocosmos reports **138,831** pickups in 2020 of 949,353 light vehicles [S5].

Nissan’s share of light pickups was **64%** with 14 versions in the Transporte.mx piece; Ford said Ranger sold **9,000** in 2015 [S2]. Nissan said **151,755 NP300 units in 2011–2014 combined** [S6].

### 2008

Autocosmos’s AMIA year-end recap: among the national top 10, **Nissan Chasis largo 25,782**; the text says it and Tsuru were the only models that did not fall in 2008, and that **in Pick Up the Chasis Largo led** [S7]. **GM Silverado 2500 (17,101)** is also in that top 10 but is full-size/HD, out of midsize scope [S7]. Complete midsize 2nd–5th for 2008 were not published in sources fetched.

### 2009–2012

No complete model ranking was retrieved. Nissan remained the volume leader in the work-pickup class (CIVAC NP300 family) [S6][S2]. Hilux, Ranger and L200 are named as the Japanese/Ford chase pack in later 2015–2016 press, but **annual units for 2009–2012 were not found**.

### 2013

Autocosmos top 10 (AMIA/AMDA): **Nissan Chasis Largo 24,994**, 9th among all light vehicles [S8]. That is the only pickup in that top 10. Hilux/Ranger/L200 volumes for 2013 were not in the fetched pages.

### 2014

Nissan’s year-end: **NP300 57,449**, the brand’s best-selling product in Mexico [S9]. Autocosmos’s 2014 “top 10 cars” article lists passenger-car leaders and brand totals, not a pickup ranking [S10]. Midsize 2nd–5th for 2014 were not retrieved as a table.

### 2015

Autocosmos national top 30 (AMIA/AMDA), pickup lines only [S11]:

| Rank among these lines | Model | Units |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (combined NP300 family) | NP300 Chasis Largo 31,700 + Doble Cabina 12,424 + Frontier 10,143 + Estacas 7,987 | **62,254** |
| 2 | Toyota Hilux | 10,454 |
| 3 | Ford Ranger | 10,382 |
| 4 | RAM 700 | 8,159 |

Autocosmos’s April 2016 comparison says **62,164** NP300 units in 2015, about **6-to-1 vs Hilux and Ranger** [S12]. The 90-unit gap versus the summed top-30 rows is unresolved. Ford’s 9,000 Ranger figure in Transporte.mx [S2] is **~1,400 below** Autocosmos’s 10,382; treat Autocosmos as the AMIA table and Ford’s remark as approximate. A fifth midsize ladder-frame truck (L200, Colorado, Amarok, Tacoma) does not appear in that top 30.

### 2016

TyT (AMIA) [S3]: Nissan **Chasis Largo 23,517**, **NP300 23,357**, **Frontier 14,526**; **Hilux 13,644**; **RAM 700 11,940**. Nissan **stopped reporting Chasis Largo and Frontier from October 2016** “por intereses propios,” per AMIA via TyT [S3]. Autocosmos top 30 matches those split rows and adds **NP300 Doble Cabina 10,698**; Ranger drops out of the top 30 [S13]. Autocosmos’s 2017 recap restates 2016 **NP300 as 23,357** (the unified label now matching only the old “NP300/Estacas” row, not the full family) [S14]. Autodato lists **Nissan Pick-up 59,644** as 4th among all models in 2016 [S15]. **These 2016 NP300 figures cannot be added naively across sources.**

**Sales-style top 5 among published pickup lines, 2016 (TyT/Autocosmos splits):** Nissan work lines (if summed, well above others), Hilux, RAM 700, then Frontier as a separate luxury-work line. Ranger is not in the top 30.

### 2017

Autocosmos: **Nissan NP300 75,324** (2016 comparison column 23,357 — a reporting unification, which Motorpasión describes as **+222.5%**) [S14][S16]. **Hilux 19,132**. **RAM 700 9,815**. El Financiero, AMDA Q1 2017 work-truck share: NP300 **38.5%**, Hilux **9.4%**, RAM 700 **5.2%**, Ford Lobo Crew Cab **4.6%** (full-size), Chevrolet Tornado **4.1%** [S17]. T21 later gives 2018 Ranger **5,900** as the base for a 2019 comparison, implying Ranger was well behind Hilux in 2017–2018 [S4].

**Pickup-relevant top 5 (2017, mixed classes):** NP300, Hilux, RAM 700, then Tornado/Lobo/Ranger — only the first three are pinned with full-year units in the Autocosmos top 30.

### 2018

Autocosmos: **NP300 67,052**, **Hilux 18,943**, **RAM 700 9,797** [S18]. T21 (INEGI): Ranger Crew Cab **5,900**, L200 **5,838**, Tacoma **4,644** [S4].

**Reconstructed 2018 top 5 excluding full-size:** NP300, Hilux, RAM 700, Ranger, L200 (Tacoma 6th in that T21 set).

### 2019

T21 (INEGI) [S4]: NP300 **62,989** (−6.06% vs 67,052), Hilux DC **17,654**, Ranger Crew Cab **9,148** (+55% vs 5,900), L200 **6,665**, Tacoma **5,516**. Those five were **52.74%** of 193,332 pickups.

Autocosmos’s 2020 article reprints 2019 alongside 2020 and shows additional lines: **Saveiro 8,890**, **RAM 700 7,483**, **RAM ST Crew Cab 8,068** (full-size), **Tornado 3,717**, **Amarok 2,555**, **Colorado DC 1,578**, **NP300 Frontier 850** [S5].

**2019 top 5 if all pickups except full-size:** NP300, Hilux, Ranger, Saveiro, RAM 700.  
**2019 top 5 if ladder-frame midsize only:** NP300, Hilux, Ranger, L200, Tacoma (T21’s cut).

### 2020

Autocosmos [S5] and Alianza Flotillera (AMDA) [S19] agree on the head of the table:

| Place | Model | 2020 | 2019 (Autocosmos) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nissan NP300 | 44,577 | 62,989 |
| 2 | Toyota Hilux | 13,666 | 17,654 |
| 3 | RAM 700 | 8,011 | 7,483 |
| 4 | VW Saveiro | 6,957 | 8,890 |
| 5 | Ford Ranger Crew Cab | 6,560 | 9,148 |

Alianza Flotillera: those five were **59.2%** of pickup sales; NP300 **28.9%** [S19]. Next in-scope: Mitsubishi L200 **5,134**, Renault Oroch **5,065**, Toyota Tacoma **4,784**, Chevrolet Tornado **2,808**, VW Amarok **1,707**, Chevrolet Colorado DC **1,129**, NP300 Frontier **409** [S5].

## Open gaps / uncertainty

- **2009, 2010, 2011, 2012:** no complete top-5 model table found in this session. Nissan family leadership is supported; order of Hilux vs Ranger vs L200 vs Tornado is not.
- **2014:** Nissan NP300 57,449 is brand-primary; rivals’ units missing.
- **2016 NP300:** TyT split vs Autocosmos unified vs Autodato 59,644 — do not treat as one number.
- T21’s 2019 “top 5” omits Saveiro and RAM 700, which Autocosmos ranks above L200/Tacoma.
- INEGI open microdata by model for 2008–2020 was not successfully downloaded (interactive tabulados empty in fetch; older year PDFs were HTML stubs).
