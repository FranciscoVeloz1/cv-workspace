# q03: What engines, horsepower ratings, and transmissions were offered on those Mexican-market midsize pickups across 2008–2020?

**Status:** researched — pending validation
**Source types to prioritize:** Official MX spec sheets, Autocosmos ficha técnica, manufacturer press

## Findings

Mexican-market horsepower and gearbox figures often differ from US, Brazilian, or Australian catalogs. The figures below are from Mexican press kits, Autocosmos tests, and one Nissan México spec sheet. Do not mix them with later 2021–2026 catalogs.

### Nissan NP300 (work family) and NP300 Frontier

Nissan’s 2012 Mexican spec sheet lists gasoline **KA24DEN 2,389 cc, 143 hp @ 5,200 rpm, 154 lb-ft @ 4,000 rpm**, 5-speed manual, rear-wheel drive on gasoline body styles; diesel **YD25DDTi 2,488 cc turbo-intercooler, 131 hp @ 3,600 rpm, 224 lb-ft @ 2,000 rpm**, also 5-speed manual, with 4x2 or part-time 4x4 depending on body [S1]. Payload on that sheet ranges from **1,490 kg** (gasoline chassis) down to **865 kg** (diesel double cab 4x4) [S1]. An Autocosmos classified listing for a 2014 2.4L chassis repeats **143 hp / 154 lb-ft, 5-speed manual, RWD** [S2].

By the April 2016 Autocosmos comparison, the **NP300 Frontier** tester used a **2.5-litre four-cylinder, 158 hp and 172 lb-ft, 6-speed manual, RWD**, with **1,096 kg** payload [S3]. Guía Autométrica’s 2016 NP300 used-price rows still list work chassis/estacas/regular cab as **TM5** and double-cab S/SE as **TM6**, so the 6-speed was not universal across the Mexican family in 2016 [S4].

### Toyota Hilux

Autocosmos’s August 2015 launch of the eighth-generation Hilux in Mexico: **2.7-litre four-cylinder, Dual VVT-i, 166 hp and 180 lb-ft (+8 hp / +2 lb-ft vs the prior Mexican 2.7), 5-speed manual, 4x2 gasoline only** at launch [S5]. Autocosmos compared that 166 hp figure with NP300 Frontier, L200 gasoline, and Ranger 2.5 [S5]. The April 2016 comparison matches **166 hp / 180 lb-ft, 5-speed manual** and **820 kg** payload on the Hilux tester—the lowest of the three trucks in that test [S3].

Diesel arrived as model-year 2018: Autocosmos reports **2.8-litre, 174 hp**; **310 lb-ft with 6-speed manual** and **334 lb-ft with 6-speed automatic**, both double-cab 4x4 with electronic rear locker [S6]. The same article’s table prices **Doble Cabina 4x4 TM $420,900** and **TA $579,900**; an embedded tweet on that page swaps those prices and says **175 hp**—treat the table and 174 hp body text as the article’s own figures, and the tweet as inconsistent [S6].

### Ford Ranger

The globally new T6 Ranger reached Mexico as **2013**, after ACE 55 delays. Autocosmos launch: **2.5 iVCT, 164 hp, 167 lb-ft, 5-speed manual, RWD**, XL/XLT double cab, payload up to **1,420 kg**; Ford said **2.2 and 3.2 diesels, automatic, and 4x4 were still to come** [S7]. The June 2013 road test repeats 164 hp / 167 lb-ft / 5MT; ABS and ESC were on the XLT tester, not described as standard on XL [S8].

Autocosmos’s September 2016 Ranger 2017 XLT diesel 4x4 test: predecessor gasoline **2.5 / 164 hp / 167 lb-ft**; added **2.2 diesel 122 hp / 223 lb-ft** and **3.2 I5 turbo-diesel 197 hp / 347 lb-ft** with **6-speed automatic** and 4x4 plus locker [S9]. Tester payload **1,095 kg** on 4x4 diesel (other Ranger versions “average 1,350 kg”); combined consumption about **9 km/l**; Argentine-built [S9]. The same test quotes both **$532,150** (lead) and **$523,150** (later)—unresolved [S9].

### Mitsubishi L200

Autocosmos (July 2015) on the fifth-generation L200 in Mexico: gasoline **2.4 / 126 hp / 143 lb-ft**, diesel **2.5 turbo / 134 hp / 232 lb-ft**, **5-speed manual either way**; gasoline 4x2 **$309,900**, diesel 4x4 **$379,900**; payload **1,065 kg gasoline / 1,040 kg diesel** [S10]. Motorpasión México matches those hp/torque/gearbox figures, ABS + two airbags, and the same two list prices [S11]. Thai-built per Autocosmos [S10]. Revista TyT (Oct 2012) already described a Mexican midsize L200 gasoline and diesel double cab with payload up to **1,025 kg** and maker claims of frontal airbags plus a rear child-seat anchor [S15]. A Motorpasión diesel-drive URL appeared in search results with a $369,900 diesel price; that page was not fetched, so it is not used here.

### Chevrolet Colorado

GM México 2018 dealer catalog (Fame Lázaro Cárdenas PDF): **2.5 I4 SIDI 200 hp @ 6,300 rpm, 191 lb-ft @ 4,400 rpm, 6-speed automatic, 4x2** (packages A/B); **3.6 V6 SIDI 308 hp @ 6,800 rpm, 275 lb-ft @ 4,000 rpm, 8-speed automatic, 4x4** (package C) [S12]. Length **5,403 mm**; payload **652 kg (4x2) / 695 kg (4x4)**—well below the one-tonne class of Hilux/Ranger/L200/Frontier [S12]. StabiliTrak, four-wheel discs, curtain airbags [S12]. Autocosmos’s Ranger test notes Colorado V6 beats Ranger on horsepower and loses on torque [S9].

### Volkswagen Amarok

Mexican launch as **2011** (coverage August 2010): **2.0 bi-turbo diesel, 163 hp, 400 Nm**, **6-speed manual**, Startline 4x2 / Highline 4x2 / Highline 4x4; ABS, ESC, electronic locker; from **$319,000** [S13]. Autocosmos later dates V6 TDI to **2016** and the first automatic to about three years after the original manual launch [S14].

2018 Highline range (Autocosmos launch): **2.0 TDI 180 hp / 295 lb-ft** or **3.0 V6 TDI**; launch text **225 hp / 368 lb-ft before 1,500 rpm**; both **8-speed automatic and 4Motion only** (no manual, no 4x2); **$650,000 / $780,000** [S16]. Motorpasión México: **2.0 180 hp / 295 lb-ft** and **3.0 224 hp / 368 lb-ft**, 8AT 4Motion, four airbags, ESC, ~1 tonne payload, same prices [S17]. Autocosmos’s February 2018 road test of the V6: **224 hp @ 4,500 rpm, 369 lb-ft from 1,250–3,000 rpm**, 8AT, 4Motion; tester priced **$825,000** [S14]. **224 vs 225 hp** and **368 vs 369 lb-ft** conflict across those 2018 pages; **$780,000 list vs $825,000 tester** also conflict [S14][S16][S17].

### Toyota Tacoma

Sold in Mexico since **2005**, built in Tijuana [S18]. Third generation (MY2016): **3.5 V6, 278 hp, 265 lb-ft, 6-speed automatic**; Motorpasión states the US **2.7 I4 was not offered in Mexico** then [S18]. Autocosmos: **+42 hp vs previous Mexican Tacoma**; seven airbags, VSC; TRD 4x2 **$452,100**, TRD 4x4 **$491,900**, special 4x4 **$539,200** [S19].

### Compact pickups mixed into Mexican “pickup” tables (not global midsize)

**Volkswagen Saveiro 2017** (on sale July 2016): **1.6, 101 hp / 105 lb-ft** (Cross **110 / 113**), **5-speed manual, FWD**; Startline from **$182,000**; payload **607–712 kg** [S20][S21]. **RAM 700** (Fiat Strada rebadge): Motor1 on the **2021** Latin-American truck (sold in Mexico) lists **1.4 / 84 hp** (SLT/Big Horn) and **1.3T / 98 hp** (Laramie), **5-speed manual, FWD**, payload up to **750 kg** [S22]. That is a 2021 debut write-up, not a 2015–2020 Mexican catalog.
