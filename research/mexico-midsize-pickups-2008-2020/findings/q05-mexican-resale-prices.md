# q05: What are typical 2026 Mexican used/resale prices for 2008–2020 examples of these trucks?

**Status:** researched — pending validation
**Source types to prioritize:** Autocosmos Guía Autométrica, classified asking prices

## Findings

Guía Autométrica figures reprinted by Autocosmos (updated **August 2026** unless noted) are **guide values**, not asking prices. Classifieds on the same pages are often **higher**. Early years (2008–2014) often have **no Autométrica row** in the URLs tried.

### Nissan NP300

**2016** Autométrica [S1]: chassis cab TM5 **$184,300**; estacas **$191,100**; double-cab S TM6 **$194,300**; regular cab PU **$194,400**; double-cab SE **$203,600**. Same page listing: 2016 DC **$310,000** (106,000 km).

**2020** Autométrica [S2]: chassis TM6 **$238,200**; estacas **$243,100**; regular PU **$243,500**; chassis TD **$244,700**; DC S **$255,200**; DC SE **$264,900**. Asking examples on that page: 2020 DC around **$339,000–$370,000**; a 2020 2.4 DC 4x4 **$349,000**.

A 2014 2.4 chassis listing: **$165,000**, 184,000 km [S3].

### Toyota Hilux

**2016** Autométrica [S4]: chassis **$191,900**; regular cab **$194,900**; DC Base **$218,700**; DC Mid **$237,100**. Listings: 2016 DC Base **$295,000**; 2016 DC SR **$329,000**.

**2020** Autométrica [S5]: regular **$264,500**; chassis **$264,800**; DC Base **$298,000**; DC SR **$323,100**; diesel 4x4 MT **$341,200**; diesel 4x4 AT leather **$414,500–$417,100**. Asking: 2020 DC gasoline roughly **$360,000–$419,000**; a **2018 diesel 4x4 auto** listing **$457,999**.

New 2016 MSRP at launch was **$251,900–$326,100** [S6]; new 2018 diesel **$420,900 MT / $579,900 AT** (table on that page) [S7].

### Ford Ranger

**2016** Autométrica [S8]: XL DC **$173,300**; XLT **$191,200**; Limited **$204,500**; XL diesel MT **$207,500**. One **2015 Limited** listing **$180,000**.

New 2013 XL/XLT were **$284,000 / $336,300** [S9]; 2017 XLT diesel tester about **$523k–$532k** [S10].

### Mitsubishi L200

**2016** Autométrica [S11]: gasoline DC TM5 **$190,000**; diesel 4x4 TM5 **$220,500**. New 2016: **$309,900 / $379,900** [S12].

### Chevrolet Colorado

**2016** Autométrica [S13]: 2.5 AT **$251,000** and **$269,600**; V6 4x4 **$294,200**—**above** 2016 Hilux/NP300/Ranger guide values for the same year.

### Volkswagen Amarok / Toyota Tacoma

No Autométrica table was retrieved for Amarok or Tacoma in this pass. New 2011 Amarok started at **$319,000** [S14]; new 2018 Highline **$650,000 / $780,000** with a V6 tester at **$825,000** [S15][S16]. New 2016 Tacoma TRD **$452,100–$539,200** [S17]. Motorpasión 2023: Hilux 2017–19 and Frontier Platinum 2020 under **$500,000**; smaller trucks under **$300,000** [S18].

### Pattern

Among 2016 guides, **Ranger XL is cheapest**, then NP300/L200/Hilux work, then **Colorado is the expensive outlier**. Asking prices for NP300 and Hilux **run tens of thousands above** Autométrica. 2008–2012 Autométrica pages for NP300/Hilux/Ranger were not retrieved (NP300 2014 URL 404).
