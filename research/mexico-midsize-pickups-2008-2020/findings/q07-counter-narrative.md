# q07: Using global class standing (not cheapness or Mexican volume), what is a sourced top-5 list for each year 2008–2020 among trucks sold in Mexico, with advantages, problems, and which popular Mexican trucks fail that test?

**Status:** researched — pending validation
**Source types to prioritize:** Synthesis from q01–q06; no new unsourced ranks

## Findings

**Rule (from the plan and q01/q06):** rank **globally class-leading ladder-frame midsize trucks that were sold new in Mexico that year**. Mexican sales (q02) are **context**. Compact FWD trucks and full-size trucks stay out. Globally acclaimed trucks **not sold new in Mexico 2008–2020** (D-Max, BT-50, X-Class) stay in the counter-narrative, not the table.

**How ranks were assigned (not invented scores):** Hilux is **#1 every year** on Toyota cumulative reach (17.7 million by 2017, 180+ countries, 521k in 2017) plus BSCB most country-#1s [q06 S1–S3]. Tacoma is the **North American** midsize actually built and sold in Mexico from 2005 [q06 S13–S16]. T6 Ranger is the **crash-test class leader globally from 2011** but **Mexican 2013 launch omitted airbags/ESC** [q06 S4, S5, S17]. Amarok is the **first ANCAP five-star body-on-frame ute**, with ESC on the **2011 Mexican** launch [q06 S6, S18]. L200/Triton was a **global four-star** in 2011 and a Mexican midsize with a documented 2012 catalog and a 2016 fifth-gen successor [q06 S4, S7, S19]. Colorado enters **MY2013** as a midsize also-ran (low 2019–2020 volume, q02; 2011 GoAuto three-star referred to the prior generation) [q06 S4, S21]. Years with fewer than five **sourced** Mexican-sold class trucks are **left short**—no filler estaquita.

Work **NP300 / Chasis Largo**, **RAM 700**, **Saveiro**, **Tornado**, and **Oroch** fail the test: they led or mixed into Mexican pickup **tables** (q02) but are either a local work chassis or compact FWD (q01). **NP300 Frontier D23** is Nissan’s global-ish finished midsize from the mid-2010s; Latin NCAP **4★** with a **borderline unstable** shell (q04) keeps it **below** Hilux/Amarok/Ranger-platform/Tacoma, typically **out of the top 5** when L200 and Amarok are present.

### Which popular Mexican trucks fail this test

- **Nissan NP300 work / Chasis Largo / Estacas / estaquita:** undisputed **Mexican volume** queen (q01/q02); 2012 spec **KA24DEN 143 hp** or **YD25 131 hp**, payloads up to **1,490 kg** chassis (q03); EPC 2021: **no ESC, no SRI, 2 airbags** on volume NP300 (q04). **Not** a global one-tonne class leader in NCAP/BSCB/TMC sources (q06).
- **RAM 700:** Strada-based **FWD** compact; in 2015–2020 sales top 5 with pickups (q02); Motor1 2021 **84/98 hp** (q01)—wrong product.
- **VW Saveiro:** FWD **1.6 101–110 hp**, from **$182,000** (2017) (q03); 2019–2020 sales above L200/Tacoma (q02).
- **Chevrolet Tornado / Renault Oroch:** compact; Autocosmos/Motorpasión class them below midsize (q01).
- **Ford Lobo / Silverado / RAM 1500:** full-size; out of midsize scope even when they outsell Amarok (q01/q02).
- **Isuzu D-Max, Mazda BT-50, Mercedes X-Class:** globally in the one-tonne conversation (D-Max country wins; BT-50 = T6 twin; X-Class = premium Navara) but **not sold new in Mexico 2008–2020** (q06 S8–S11).

### Used-buyer note (2026)

Autométrica **Aug 2026** (q05): among 2016 guides, **Ranger XL** is the cheap global-platform entry; **Colorado** is the expensive low-payload outlier; **NP300/Hilux** asking prices run **tens of thousands above** guide. Tacoma/Amarok **guides were not retrieved**. A “globally best” shortlist in a Mexican classifieds hunt is still **Hilux (prefer late-2019 5★ diesel DC if budget allows), Tacoma 3rd gen, Amarok with ESC, Ranger only if the truck has the later airbag/ESC pack, L200 diesel 4x4 for abuse**—not an NP300/RAM 700/Saveiro even when those are cheaper and more common.

## Open gaps / uncertainty

- **2008–2010 (and 2011) tables have fewer than five rows** because Ranger T6, Colorado, Amarok (before 2011), and year-dated L200 were not sourced as Mexican new trucks yet. Filling those slots with NP300 would violate the ranking rule.
- Pre-T6 Ranger **new** in Mexico: unconfirmed (q06).
- L200 **2008–2011** Mexican new-sale: implied, not launch-dated (q06).
- Rank **2 vs 3** (Ranger platform vs Tacoma) is a judgment: Ranger wins **global crash architecture**, Tacoma wins **MX safety pack + NA dependability study**. The table keeps Ranger **#2 from 2013** with the decontenting caveat in the cell—not as if every Mexican XL were a 5★ ANCAP truck.
- No numeric “score” combining NCAP + sales + hp was computed; years are ordinal from the same evidence stack.
