# q05: What Mexican used/resale asking prices are documented for 2010–2020 examples?

**Status:** researched — pending validation
**Source types to prioritize:** Guía Autométrica, Seminuevos, Autocosmos classifieds

## Findings

Prices below are **Guía Autométrica guide values** republished by Autocosmos, **updated August 2026**, for **model-year 2018** trucks (a mid-window year still common on the used market in 2026). They are **not** transaction prices. Classified asking prices on the same pages are separate and often higher or lower. New MSRP from 2015–2019 launches is included only as a comparison anchor.

### New-price anchors (historical MSRP, not used)

- Hilux 2016 petrol: $251,900–$326,100 [S5].
- Hilux diesel 2018 launch: $420,000 MT / $579,900 AT [S6].
- Tacoma 2016: $452,100–$539,200 [S7].
- Ranger 2020: $380,900–$624,600 (plus unquoted fleet 2.2) [S8].
- Gladiator Rubicon 2019: $1,119,900 [S9].

### Pattern in the 2018 guide snapshot

Among 2018 midsize/work trucks in these tables, **petrol NP300 and petrol Ranger XL** cluster near **$207k–$227k**; **Hilux petrol double-cab** is higher (**$258k–$281k**); **diesel 4x4 Hilux auto** and **Ranger 3.2 4x4** sit **$303k–$360k** in the guide; **Tacoma V6** is a step above (**$375k–$419k**) [S1][S2][S3][S4]. Asking ads can undershoot (high-km NP300) or overshoot (Hilux diesel auto) the guide [S1][S3].

L200, Amarok, Colorado, Lobo/F-150, Silverado, RAM 1500 Autométrica pages were not fetched.

## Open gaps / uncertainty

- Autométrica is a **trade guide**, not sold-price microdata; classifieds are asking prices.
- August 2026 vintage means these are current used values of 2018 trucks, not 2018-era used prices.
- No fetched Autométrica tables for L200, Amarok, Colorado, NP300 diesel-specific rows, Lobo, Silverado, or 2010–2015 model years.
- Kilometre adjustments in Autométrica’s mileage table were not applied; listed figures are the base version prices shown.
