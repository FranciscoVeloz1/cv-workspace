# q03: For those leaders, what engines, horsepower, and transmissions were offered in Mexico (vs the global/US truck of the same name)?

**Status:** researched — pending validation
**Source types to prioritize:** Mexican press kits, Autocosmos/Motorpasión fichas

## Findings

Mexican catalogs in this window often sold the **same badge** with **weaker or narrower** powertrains than the diesel 4x4 Hilux / Ranger / Triton known in Australia, the Middle East, or Africa. Sources below are Mexican press catalogs, not foreign brochures.

### Toyota Hilux (eighth generation in Mexico)

**Petrol launch (2016 MY), Thailand-sourced:** Autocosmos reported the same 2.7 I4 as the prior generation, now Dual VVT-i, **166 hp** and **180 lb-ft**, **5-speed manual**, and — “por el momento” — **4x2 gasoline only**. Safety on all versions: ABS and **three airbags** (two frontal + driver knee). Prices from **$251,900** chassis to **$326,100** double-cab SR [S1]. Autocosmos compared those 166 hp as 6 hp more than NP300 Frontier, 40 more than L200, and 2 more than Ranger in that article’s telling [S1].

**Diesel 4x4 (announced Nov 2017 as 2018 MY):** Motorpasión: 2.8 I4 diesel, **174 hp**, **310 lb-ft** (manual) / **334 lb-ft** (automatic), 6MT or 6AT, full-time 4x4 with electronic rear locker on the automatic; double cab only in that launch; fleet manual **$420,000**; automatic **$579,900**; still **ABS + 3 airbags** on the listed kits [S2]. T21, quoting Toyota, listed the same 174 hp @ 3,400 rpm, **310 lb-ft** @ 2,600 rpm for MT and **332 lb-ft** @ 2,400 rpm for AT (2 lb-ft lower than Motorpasión’s automatic figure) [S3]. Motorpasión stated that in most markets the requested Hilux is diesel, and that Hilux is Toyota’s best-seller in Mexico [S2]. Manufactura: first Toyota diesels in Mexico; US/Canada sell Tacoma instead of Hilux [S4].

**2019 Autocosmos catalog (petrol + diesel together):** 2.7 petrol 166 hp / 180 lb-ft, 5MT, RWD; 2.8 diesel 174 hp / 310 lb-ft, 6MT or 6AT, 4x4; payload **820–1,210 kg** [S5].

### Nissan NP300 / NP300 Frontier (Mexico work family)

Autocosmos 2019: base **2.5 I4 petrol 166 hp / 178 lb-ft**; diesel **161 hp / 297 lb-ft**; **6MT** sending petrol versions to the rear wheels; diesel **4x4**; top trim **7-speed automatic**; payload **901–1,525 kg**; base safety ABS/EBD + frontal airbags; top Frontier can add VDC, hill assist, camera [S5]. Motorpasión (Mar 2020) said all NP300 variants offer only **two airbags** and ABS, with ESC only on some versions [S6].

### Nissan Frontier V6 (US-style, separate line)

Autocosmos 2019: **4.0 V6 261 hp / 281 lb-ft**, **5AT**, payload **590 kg**, tow up to 2,858 kg; six airbags, ABS/EBD, VDC, traction control [S5].

### Ford Ranger (T6)

Autocosmos 2019: 2.5 petrol **164 hp / 167 lb-ft**, **5MT**; 3.2 I5 diesel **197 hp / 347 lb-ft**, **6AT**; all but the cheapest with 4x4; payload up to **1,270 kg**; Autocosmos claimed **seven airbags + ESC from the cheapest Ranger** [S5]. Motorpasión Ranger 2020 (6 Aug 2019): 2.5 petrol 164 hp / 167 lb-ft, 5MT, 4x2; 3.2 diesel 197 hp / 347 lb-ft, 6AT, 4x4; **fleet** 2.2 diesel **150 hp / 284 lb-ft**, 6MT, 4x4; payload up to **1.3 t**; side airbags on all 2020 versions plus frontal and driver knee; prices XL 2.5 **$380,900**, XLT 2.5 **$427,500**, XLT 3.2 4x4 **$624,600** [S7].

### Mitsubishi L200

Autocosmos 2019: 2.4 petrol **126 hp / 143 lb-ft**, RWD; “cuatro litros turbodiésel” in the same paragraph listing **134 hp / 232 lb-ft**, 4x4 — the displacement wording is internally inconsistent with a 2.4-class diesel (see Open gaps); **5MT** either way; payload up to **1,065 kg**; ABS/EBD, two frontal airbags, TPMS [S5].

### Toyota Tacoma (built in Tijuana)

Autocosmos 2019: **3.5 V6 278 hp / 265 lb-ft**, **6AT**, 4x2 or 4x4, payload **682 kg**; six airbags, ABS/EBD/BA, traction and stability control [S5]. Autocosmos Tacoma 2016 launch: 3.5 V6 **278 hp** (+42 vs prior gen) / 265 lb-ft, 6AT with sequential mode; Star Safety System (ABS, TRAC, VSC, EBD); **seven airbags** including knee; TRD 4x2 **$452,100**, TRD 4x4 **$491,900**, special edition 4x4 **$539,200** [S8]. (2019 catalog says six airbags; 2015 launch says seven — conflict.)

### Volkswagen Amarok

Autocosmos 2019: diesel only, double cab; entry **2.0 TDI 140 hp / 184 lb-ft**, 6MT, 4x2 or 4x4, payload **1,151 kg**; mid 2.0 TDI **180 hp / 218 lb-ft**; top “**V6 de 2.0 Litros TDI**” **224 hp / 272 lb-ft** [S5]. The “V6 de 2.0” label is almost certainly a press error for the 3.0 V6 TDI used on Amarok globally; the 224 hp figure is consistent with that V6, not a 2.0 (see Open gaps). ESC and hill assist only on costlier trims in that catalog; entry has frontal airbags, ABS, traction control, electronic diff lock [S5].

### Chevrolet Colorado

Autocosmos 2019: 2.5 **200 hp / 191 lb-ft**, 6AT, 4x2; 3.6 **308 hp / 275 lb-ft**, 8AT, 4x4; payload **695 kg**; six airbags (front, side, curtain), ABS, traction, ESC [S5].

### Jeep Gladiator (from 2019)

Motorpasión: only **3.6 Pentastar 285 hp / 260 lb-ft**, **8AT**, start-stop; Rubicon 4x4 Rock-Trac; payload **720 kg**, tow 3,470 kg; launch price **$1,119,900** [S9]. Autocosmos preview matched 285 hp / 260 lb-ft / 8AT and ~725 kg payload [S5].

### Contrast: global vs Mexican Hilux

Toyota Mexico (via T21) said Hilux is sold in 180+ countries with 17.3 million cumulative units [S3]. Motorpasión said the usual global configuration is diesel [S2]. Mexico’s volume 2016 truck was petrol 2.7 / 5MT / 4x2 [S1] until diesel 4x4 arrived as 2018 MY [S2][S3][S4]. US/Canada do not get Hilux [S4].

## Open gaps / uncertainty

- Official Toyota/Ford/Nissan **PDF fichas** for each year 2010–2020 were not fetched; figures are 2015–2020 press catalogs.
- Hilux automatic diesel torque: Motorpasión **334 lb-ft** [S2] vs T21 **332 lb-ft** [S3].
- Amarok “V6 de 2.0 TDI 224 hp” is internally inconsistent [S5]; treat 224 hp as the V6 peak and 2.0 as likely a typo for 3.0.
- L200 diesel displacement wording “cuatro litros turbodiésel” vs 134 hp [S5] is inconsistent with known 2.4 diesels; power/torque/5MT/4x4 are what the catalog clearly states.
- Tacoma airbag count: seven in 2015 launch [S8] vs six in 2019 catalog [S5].
- Full-size Lobo/Silverado/RAM 1500 Mexican engine grids for 2010–2020 were not fetched as manufacturer fichas.
- Pre-2015 (seventh-gen Hilux, prior Ranger) Mexican hp/transmission tables were not fetched.
