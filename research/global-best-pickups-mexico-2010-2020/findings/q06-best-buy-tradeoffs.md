# q06: On “best globally but buyable in Mexico” plus cheap-and-durable criteria, which Mexican-offered pickups look strongest, and what are documented advantages and problems?

**Status:** researched — pending validation
**Source types to prioritize:** Combined specs + sales + safety + used prices

## Findings

This question synthesizes q01–q05. It is **not** a personal VIN recommendation. “Strongest” is framed against the user’s criterion: **global reputation + actually sold new in Mexico 2010–2020**, then Mexican sales, Mexican spec, safety, theft, and 2026 used guide prices.

### What “global best, Mexican offering” actually was

Volume in Mexico among that set was **Nissan NP300**, by a wide margin every year with complete data (2015–2020), including 75,324 unified units in 2017 and 67,052 in 2018 [S1][S2][S3]. That is **Mexican work-truck leadership**, with Autocosmos tracing Datsun pickups to 1959 and Mexico-specific body mix [S4] — not the same thing as winning Latin NCAP or matching Australian diesel Hilux spec.

The globally storied **Hilux** was consistently **#2** among this set when NP300 is unified (2017–2020) and close to Ranger in 2015 (10,454 vs 10,382) [S5][S1][S2][S6]. Toyota said 17.3 million Hilux in 180+ countries and that 2016 Mexico volume made it the 10th Hilux market [S7]. Mexican **product** at 2016 launch was petrol 2.7 / 166 hp / 5MT / **4x2 only**, ABS + 3 airbags [S8]; diesel 4x4 arrived as 2018 MY [S9][S7].

**Ranger** was the other global T6 icon with real Mexican volume (10,382 in 2015; 9,148 in 2019; 6,560 in 2020) [S5][S2][S6]. Autocosmos 2019 attributed 7 airbags + ESC from the cheapest Ranger and ~1.27 t payload [S4]; Motorpasión 2020 Ranger listed 3.2 diesel 197 hp / 347 lb-ft 6AT 4x4 and fleet 2.2 150 hp [S10]. Ranger is **absent** from Motorpasión’s January 2020 no-ESC list that **includes Hilux, L200, and NP300 (ESC optional)** [S11].

**L200** made the Mexican top 5 of this set in 2019–2020 (6,665 / 5,134) [S2][S6] with a weak Mexican petrol **126 hp** / diesel **134 hp** 5MT catalog and no ESC in the 2019 kit [S4][S11] — a Triton legend badge on a decontented truck.

**Tacoma** (Mexico-built, US-oriented V6, 278 hp, 6AT, ESC, payload 682 kg) sold 4,644–5,516 in 2018–2019 [S3][S2][S4] — lifestyle, not work volume. **Full-size** Silverado/Lobo/RAM Crew Cab appear in the 2018–2020 top 5 of this set depending on year and whether cab lines are split [S3][S2][S6]. **Amarok** stayed low-volume diesel double-cab (1,851–2,555) [S3][S2]. **Gladiator** arrived 2019 at $1.12 million, 394 then 1,325 units [S12][S6]. **D-Max** — a global durability icon — was **not** a 2010–2020 Mexican retail pickup [S13].

### Documented advantages (by nameplate, Mexican offering)

| Nameplate | Documented advantages in Mexico |
|---|---|
| NP300 | Overwhelming parts/body availability; payload up to 1,525 kg in 2019 catalog [S4]; cheapest 2018 Autométrica work truck (~$207k–$233k) [S14]; diesel 161 hp / 297 lb-ft and 4x4 diesel in catalog [S4]. |
| Hilux | Global sales/durability claim [S7]; Latin NCAP **5★** on Jul 2019+ 7-airbag+ESC Double Cab/SW4 [S15]; diesel 174 hp 4x4 from 2018 MY [S9]; strong used values for diesel auto ($360,400 guide; ads to $457,999) [S16]. |
| Ranger | Global T6 diesel torque 347 lb-ft [S4][S10]; ESC/airbag catalog better than base Hilux/L200 in 2019–2020 press [S4][S11]; payload ~1.3 t [S10]; 2018 3.2 4x4 guide $303,500 vs Hilux diesel auto $360,400 [S17][S16]. |
| Tacoma | Built in Tijuana [S18]; V6 278 hp, ESC, 6–7 airbags [S4][S18]; unique Hilux+Tacoma market [S4]. |
| Colorado | 308 hp 3.6 / 8AT, 6 airbags + ESC [S4]. |
| Gladiator | Open-air Jeep 4x4, 285 hp 8AT, ADAS on Rubicon [S12]. |

### Documented problems

| Nameplate | Documented problems |
|---|---|
| NP300 | AMIS **most-stolen** 2019 (4,657) and still 2,731 in 2020 [S19][S20]; Latin NCAP **4★**, 2 airbags, **borderline unstable** structure on the tested DC [S15]; ESC **optional** [S11][S21]. Volume ≠ crash or theft outcome. |
| Hilux | 2016–early Mexican truck is **not** the global diesel 4x4 [S8][S9]; Motorpasión 2020 still lists Hilux **without ESC** [S11]; 2016 safety 3 airbags [S8]; **548 violent thefts** in 2020 AMIS violent top five [S20]. |
| L200 | 126/134 hp, 5MT, no ESC [S4][S11]. |
| Tacoma / Colorado / Frontier V6 | Payloads 590–695 kg [S4] — below work NP300/Ranger. |
| Amarok | ESC not on entry trims; “V6 2.0” catalog wording unreliable [S4]; low volume. |
| Gladiator | Price in Lobo/RAM 1500 territory [S12]; tiny 2019–2020 volume [S6]. |

### Cheap-and-durable **within this global-badge set** (not compact unibody)

- **Lowest 2018 guide / parts-common work truck:** NP300 chassis/SE (~$207k–$233k) [S14], accepting theft and 2-airbag/optional-ESC safety [S15][S11][S19].
- **If the criterion is “closest to the world Hilux”:** Mexican **diesel 4x4 Hilux from 2018 MY**, not 2016 petrol 4x2 [S8][S9]; used guide $284k MT / $360k AT [S16]; confirm ESC/airbags on the specific VIN against the July 2019 Latin NCAP cutoff [S15][S11].
- **If the criterion is “global midsize with Mexican ESC catalog + diesel torque without Hilux diesel price”:** **Ranger 3.2 4x4** (guide $303,500 for 2018 XLT 3.2) [S17][S10][S11].
- **If the criterion is US-style V6 + ESC, pay more, haul less:** Tacoma [S4][S18][S22].

El Financiero attributed Nissan work-truck share partly to **Súbete** financing for informal small business, not only product quality [S23].

## Open gaps / uncertainty

- No independent Mexican reliability ranking to convert “indestructible” into a failure rate.
- Best-buy depends on whether the buyer weights payload/theft (NP300), diesel 4x4 authenticity (Hilux 2018+), or ESC catalog (Ranger).
- Full-size F-150/Lobo/Silverado Mexican spec and used prices were not fetched, so they are not ranked as used buys here despite 2018 sales presence [S3].
