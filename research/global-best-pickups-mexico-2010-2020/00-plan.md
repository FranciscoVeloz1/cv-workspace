# Research Plan: Globally Best Pickups Sold in Mexico, 2010–2020

## Objective
Identify which pickups with a **global** reputation for quality, durability, or class leadership were actually sold new in Mexico from 2010 through 2020; rank the top 5 of that set by Mexican sales each year; document Mexican-market engines, horsepower, and transmissions; used/resale prices in Mexico; and advantages versus documented problems — including where the Mexican product is a weaker or different truck than the global icon. This supports a used-truck purchase decision that prefers world-class nameplates available locally, not the compact unibody class researched previously.

## Scope
**In scope:**
- Pickups sold **new** in Mexico, calendar years / model years **2010–2020**, that belong to nameplates with documented **international** standing (global sales leadership, durability reputation, independent crash/reliability tests, or class-defining status). Expected candidates pending sources: Toyota Hilux, Toyota Tacoma, Ford Ranger, Ford F-150/Lobo, Chevrolet Silverado/Cheyenne, RAM 1500, Nissan NP300/Navara/Frontier, Mitsubishi L200, Volkswagen Amarok, Chevrolet S10/Colorado, GMC Sierra, Jeep Gladiator (if on sale in-window).
- Year-by-year **top 5 Mexican sales** among that globally regarded set (not among compact unibody trucks; not a padded mix of Tornado/Saveiro unless a source treats them as globally class-leading).
- Mexican-market technical specs (engine, hp, transmission) — which often differ from US/EU/ANZ versions.
- Mexican used/resale asking or guide prices as observed in this session.
- Reliability, safety, common problems, theft, and decontenting relative to the same nameplate abroad.

**Out of scope:**
- Compact FWD unibody pickups (Chevrolet Tornado, VW Saveiro, Fiat Strada/RAM 700, Renault Oroch) except as a one-line contrast that they were **not** selected as globally best-in-class.
- Pickups never retailed in Mexico (e.g. US-only trims, Isuzu D-Max / Mazda BT-50 / Honda Ridgeline unless a source proves Mexican retail).
- Years after 2020 except to date a late-2020 facelift or current used asking prices for 2010–2020 trucks.
- A personal VIN recommendation or financing quote.

## Source policy
Prioritize official/primary sources. Reputable secondary sources allowed for corroboration
and context. Exclude unattributed or unverifiable sources. No invented data or citations
anywhere in this project.

Default go-to types: INEGI RAIAVL / AMIA / AMDA sales tables; manufacturer Mexican press kits and fichas; Autocosmos / Motorpasión México / Indicador Automotriz; Toyota/Ford/Nissan global durability or sales claims only when tied to the Mexican product; Latin NCAP / Global NCAP; PROFECO where available; Guía Autométrica / Seminuevos / Autocosmos classifieds for **asking** used prices. Wikipedia only as a pointer, never as the sole source for a figure. J.D. Power / Consumer Reports apply to **US** Tacoma/F-150/Silverado — label that as US evidence, not Mexican IQS, unless a Mexican study is fetched.
