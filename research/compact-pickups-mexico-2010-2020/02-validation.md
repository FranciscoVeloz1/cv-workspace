# Validation Report: Compact Pickups in Mexico, 2010–2020

## Summary
Core unit figures for 2018–2020, first-gen RAM 700 / Saveiro 2017 / Oroch 2018 / Tornado 1.8 specs, Motorpasión ESC list, Latin NCAP 2022, Guía Autométrica Tornado/Saveiro/Oroch residuals, and T21/INEGI all-pickup contrast were re-fetched and match the findings. Gaps for 2010–2014 unit tables, RAM 700 Autométrica, and mechanical failure rates are already labelled as gaps, not invented numbers. Isolated issues: Wikipedia as sole support for Courier’s last Mexican year; a later RAM PDF that lists 1.4 Fire (already treated as a conflict); Latin NCAP 2022 rating a post-window generation. **Ready for redaction as-is**, provided the redactor keeps those caveats and does not pad a fifth compact nameplate.
