# q05: What Mexican used/resale asking prices are documented for 2010–2020 examples of these models?

**Status:** researched — pending validation
**Source types to prioritize:** Kavak, Seminuevos.com, Autocosmos classifieds, Mexican used-car guides

## Findings

Prices below are **Guía Autométrica residual values and classified asking prices observed 5 Sep 2026**, not notarized sale prices. Autométrica pages fetched for this session are labelled **“Precios actualizados a agosto de 2026.”** Classifieds are individual ads and can sit well above or below the guide.

Rough 2026 resale band for a 2015–2020 Tornado from this evidence: about **$120,000–$250,000** depending on year, trim, kilometres and whether one uses the guide or a dealer ask.

### RAM 700 — Seminuevos portal valuations + classified asking prices

Guía Autométrica URLs tried under `ram/700/2019` and `ram-comerciales/700/2019` returned a **brand picker with no RAM 700 rows** [S7][S16]. Seminuevos.com’s own listing-based cotizador (not Guía Autométrica) did return 2019 and 2020 bands [S17][S18]:

| Model year | Seminuevos “precio ideal” | Lowest listed | Highest listed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2019 | $257,242 | $193,000 | $329,500 [S17] |
| 2020 | $249,800 | $192,000 | $272,200 [S18] |

The 2020 “ideal” sitting **below** 2019 is a portal-statistic quirk, not a claim that 2020 trucks are worth less as a rule. Individual ads on those pages and Autocosmos [S4][S5][S17][S18]:

- RAM 700 Club Cab Adventure **2019**, 140,000 km, Tlaquepaque, **$222,000** [S17]
- RAM 700 Club Cab Adventure **2019**, 155,000 km, Querétaro, **$205,000** [S5]
- RAM 700 Club Cab Adventure **2020**, 72,913 km, San Pedro Garza García, **$254,000** [S4][S18]
- RAM 700 1.6 Club Cab Adventure **2020**, 69,000 km, Guadalajara, **$269,000** [S18]
- RAM 700 Club Cab Adventure **2018**, 23,711 km, listed around **$370,000** (low-km outlier) [S4]

New RAM 700 still advertised “from **$334,900**” on Autocosmos used pages [S4] — that is a **current new** floor, not a 2015–2020 residual. Late-2020 **new** 2021 RAM 700 prices were SLT Regular **$244,900** through Laramie **$334,900** [S6]. A 2020 used Adventure at $254,000 sits near that old new-SLT range and below current new stickers.

Guide Oroch residuals are **higher than equivalent-year Tornado LS** and **in the same peso neighbourhood as RAM 700 Seminuevos “ideal”** once dealer ads ($245k–$339k) are used instead of the guide floor.

**New** (not resale) context: Saveiro 2017 **$182,000–$327,000** [S9]; Oroch 2018 launch **$269,900–$328,900** [S10]; Seminuevos Feb 2020 list **$280,500–$343,100** [S11].

### Original new prices (to interpret depreciation, not 2026 resale)

- Tornado 2011.5 packages: **$162,500 / $175,500 / $195,200** [S13]
- RAM 700 2015: **$189,900 / $249,900** [S14]
- Saveiro 2017: **$182,000–$327,000** [S9]
- Oroch 2018: **$269,900–$328,900** [S10]

A 2015 Tornado guide value around **$120,000–$135,000** in 2026 [S1] versus ~$163,000–$195,000 new in 2011.5 [S13] is a **long, work-truck-style** residual, not a collector premium. A 2020 Tornado LT asking **$248,000** [S3] versus 2018–2019 new Tornado prices in the mid-$200,000s to ~$305,000 [S15] shows **strong asking** on low-km late trucks.

### How to use these numbers

- Prefer **Guía Autométrica** for Tornado, Saveiro and Oroch as a **standardized residual**, then check ads (Oroch ads in this session sit **above** the guide).
- RAM 700: use Seminuevos 2019/2020 bands (~$192k–$330k) plus ads; the $370,000 / 23,711 km 2018 is a **low-km outlier**.
- Across the class, 2017–2020 **work cabs** (Tornado LS, Saveiro Starline/Robust) cluster about **$120,000–$180,000** on the guide; **Club Cab / Pepper / Oroch** cluster about **$190,000–$260,000** guide, with dealer asks often **$245,000–$270,000**.

## Open gaps / uncertainty

- Guía Autométrica for RAM 700 2010–2020 was not retrieved (slug not found); Seminuevos listing stats used instead.
- Saveiro 2018 fetch returned only the Starline row; Highline/Trendline/Cross 2018 residuals not in that extract.
- Oroch 2020 fetch returned Zen and Outsider TM6 only; Intens/auto 2020 residual not in that extract.
- No Kavak 2010–2020 compact-pickup ask sample with prices in the fetch.
- Guide values are not conditioned on a stated odometer in the HTML table (the page offers km buckets in the UI but did not bind a single km to the peso figure in the extract).
- One 2018 RAM 700 at $370,000 / 23,711 km would distort any “average.”
- Inflation-adjusted real depreciation was not computed.
