# q03: For those leading models, what engines, horsepower, and transmissions were offered in Mexico across 2010–2020 (including mid-cycle changes)?

**Status:** researched — pending validation
**Source types to prioritize:** Official Mexican press kits, Autocosmos/Motorpasión specs, manufacturer catalogs

## Findings

All of these compact pickups are **gasoline, front-wheel-drive, unibody** tools. None of the Mexican launches fetched for this class offered a diesel in 2010–2020. Payloads cluster around **650–750 kg**, far below Nissan NP300-class work trucks.

### Chevrolet Tornado (through 2020)

Mexican dealer and press specs for the 2010s Tornado are stable: **1.8-litre four-cylinder**, **105 hp at 5,400 rpm**, **119 lb-ft at 3,000 rpm**, **five-speed manual only** [S1][S2][S3]. Chevrolet Acuña’s Tornado page lists combined fuel use about **15.5–16 km/l** depending on trim, payload **734 kg**, hydraulic steering, front discs / rear drums with ABS, dual front airbags [S1]. Vision Automotriz (2017) repeats 1.8 L, 105 hp, 119 lb-ft, five-speed manual, up to **16 km/l** combined, **734 kg** payload [S3]. Motorpasión’s successor article uses the same 105 hp / 119 lb-ft / five-speed-manual package and 2010s prices from **247,700 to 290,800 pesos** for the then-current range [S2]. A 2017 Chevrolet brochure-style document matches **1.8 L MPFI-SFI SOHC**, 105 hp, 119 lb-ft, five-speed manual, **16 km/l** combined, **734 kg** [S4]. **No automatic** appears in these Mexican Tornado sources.

### Volkswagen Saveiro (sixth generation in this window)

Autocosmos’s 2017 Mexican launch (on-sale prices from **$182,000**):

- Startline / Highline / Trendline: **1.6 L four-cylinder, 101 hp, 105 lb-ft, 8 valves**, **five-speed manual** [S5].
- Cross: same 1.6 family, **110 hp, 113 lb-ft, 16 valves**, five-speed manual [S5].
- Payload: regular cab up to **712 kg**; extended cab **696 kg**; double cab **667 kg**; Cross **607 kg** [S5].
- From 2017, Autocosmos says **all versions** had four-wheel disc brakes, ABS, hydraulic steering and two airbags; Cross added ESC/traction, locking differential, hill-start assist [S5].

A later Mercado Libre Mexico comparison of **current** Saveiro vs RAM 700 quotes Saveiro **1.6 L, 109 hp, 114 lb-ft**, five-speed manual, **667 kg**, ~**16 km/l** [S6]. That 109 hp figure is a **post-window or later-tune** spec relative to the 101/110 hp 2017 Autocosmos split; it should not be back-dated onto every 2010–2017 Saveiro without a matching year-model sheet.

### RAM 700 / Fiat Strada — two generations inside 2010–2020

**First generation in Mexico (MY 2015 through most of 2020).** Autocosmos launch (Oct 2014): **1.6 L four-cylinder, 115 hp, 119 lb-ft**, **five-speed manual**, McPherson front / rigid axle with leaf springs rear. Regular cab payload **705 kg**; Club Cab **650 kg**. Combined consumption quoted **12.38 km/l**. Fuel tank **58 L**. Launch prices: Regular Cab **$189,900**; Club Cab **$249,900**. ABS and dual front airbags on both; Club Cab had a **front differential lock** [S7]. Motor a Diesel’s RAM table also highlighted **115 hp** and **705 kg** for the regular cab, five-speed manual, 7-year/100,000 km powertrain warranty, 15,000 km service interval [S8].

**Second generation (RAM 700 2021, on sale 18 Nov 2020).** Autocosmos: **1.3 L Firefly four-cylinder, 98 hp, 95 lb-ft**, **five-speed manual only** at launch, combined **18.3 km/l**. Regular cab **750 kg / 1,354 L**; crew cab **650 kg / 844 L**; tow rating **400 kg** all versions. ESC, traction control, hill-start assist; two airbags on regular cab, four on crew cab [S9]. Autocosmos vs Oroch repeats 1.3 L / 98 hp / 95 lb-ft / 5-speed manual / 18.3 km/l [S10].

A later RAM technical PDF circulating online lists **1.4 Fire (84 hp)** and **1.3 Firefly (98 hp)** [S11]. The **Mexican 2015 launch article specifies 1.6 L / 115 hp**, not 1.4 Fire [S7]. Treat 1.4 Fire as a **different-market or later-sheet** engine unless a Mexican MY price list for 2015–2020 first-gen 700 contradicts Autocosmos. This session did not fetch a year-by-year Mexican ficha proving a 1.4 swap before the 2021 1.3 Firefly.

### Renault Oroch (from May 2018)

Autocosmos launch: mechanics from Duster — **2.0 L, 140 hp, 142 lb-ft**, **six-speed manual or four-speed automatic** with manual mode. Payload **650 kg**. All versions ABS + dual front airbags. Prices: ZEN 6-speed manual **$269,900**; INTENS 4-speed auto **$313,900**; OUTSIDER 4-speed auto **$328,900** [S12]. Autocosmos 2020 comparison uses **140 hp and 144 lb-ft**, 6-speed manual or 4-speed auto, combined **16.1 km/l** (manual) and **14.8 km/l** (auto), 21 cm ground clearance, **710 kg** towing, **683 L** box volume, still **650 kg** payload [S10]. Seminuevos (Feb 2020) quoted the same 2.0 four-cylinder with **143 hp**, 6-speed manual or 4-speed auto, and then-current prices ZEN **$280,500** / Outsider TM **$322,700** / Outsider TA **$343,100** [S13]. **140 vs 143 hp** is a small press discrepancy; both describe the Duster 2.0, not a turbo.

Oroch’s rear **multilink independent** suspension is a documented differentiator vs leaf-sprung RAM 700 and typical compact work pickups [S12][S13].

### Ford Courier / Fiat Strada (pre-RAM)

Mexican technical sheets for 2010 Courier and 2010–2014 Strada were **not fetched**. Brazil-market Courier used a 1.6 Rocam in later years [S14]; that must not be stated as a confirmed Mexican rating without a Mexico ficha.
