# q02: What were the top 5 compact pickups in Mexico by annual sales (or the fullest available ranking) for each calendar year 2010 through 2020?

**Status:** researched — pending validation
**Source types to prioritize:** INEGI RAIAVL, AMIA, AMDA, Best Selling Cars Blog, Autocosmos year wrap-ups

## Findings

**How to read these lists.** Mexico almost never had five compact nameplates on sale at once (see q01). Rankings below are **within the compact / small unibody class**, not the national all-pickup chart. INEGI-based full-model tables published by Motorpasión México exist for **2018, 2019 and 2020**; Autocosmos published overlapping 2019–2020 pickup tables and top-30 lists that catch RAM 700 in 2015 and 2016–2017. Earlier years are thinner.

National context that is **not** a compact ranking: T21, citing INEGI, reported **193,332** pickup sales in 2019 (up 4.82% from **184,439** in 2018). Nissan NP300 led all pickups at **62,989** (down from **67,052** in 2018). The five models NP300, Hilux, Ranger Crew Cab, L200 and Tacoma were **52.74%** of all pickups [S1]. Autocosmos’s all-pickup table for 2020 put NP300 at **44,577** (2019: 62,989) with Hilux second at 13,666; RAM 700 was third **among all pickups** that year because compact volume is still large enough to outsell many midsize and full-size lines [S2].

### 2017

Autocosmos top-30 (AMIA/AMDA-era reporting): **RAM 700 9,815** (2016: 11,940) — 29th overall. Saveiro and Tornado are **absent from the top 30**, so each sold **fewer than 8,918** (Toyota Hiace, #30) [S8]. Compact ranking with a hard #1 only:

1. RAM 700 — 9,815 [S8]
2–3. Saveiro and Tornado — order not proven from a full INEGI list this year; both below 8,918.
4–5. Oroch not yet on sale (May 2018) [S6].

### 2016

TyT, citing AMIA: **RAM 700 11,940** (imported, like Hilux) [S9]. Autocosmos 2017 table repeats 11,940 for 2016 [S8]. Saveiro/Tornado again not in the 2016 top-30 column of that table (Hilux 13,644 is listed; RAM 700 is the compact that cracked the national top 30).

1. RAM 700 — 11,940 [S8][S9]
2–3. Saveiro, Tornado — units not in the fetched top-30; VW later said Saveiro had 32% of the compact segment around the 2017 model presentation [S10].
4–5. empty / Oroch not on sale.

### 2015

Autocosmos top 30: **RAM 700 8,159**, 29th overall — the **only compact pickup in the national top 30** [S11]. Best Selling Cars Blog independently reported RAM 700 (rebadged Brazilian Fiat Strada) at **8,159** and #40 in its 2015 write-up [S12]. Saveiro and Tornado therefore sold **under 7,987** (Nissan NP300 Estacas, #30) [S11].

1. RAM 700 — 8,159 [S11][S12]
2–3. Saveiro, Tornado — below that threshold; 2014 Saveiro share claim of 44% [S13] implies Saveiro was still a large compact player entering 2015, but 2015 unit order vs Tornado is **not proven** from a full table.

### 2014

VW, quoted in Motorpasión: 2014 was “the year of the Saveiro,” **44% of the compact segment**, above Tornado, RAM 700 and Fiat Strada [S13]. RAM 700 only arrived in **October** [S14], so Saveiro as segment-share leader is plausible. **No fetched INEGI/AMIA table gives 2014 unit counts for Tornado or Saveiro.** Fiat Strada still appears in overlap commentary [S13]. Numeric top 5 cannot be completed.

### 2011

AMIA figures via Expansión/CNNExpansión: among pickups, Nissan Chasis Largo **21,062** and **Chevrolet Tornado 8,034** [S15]. Tornado was the compact volume leader that year and the second-best-selling pickup nameplate in that AMIA pair. Saveiro and Strada units were not in that article.

### 2010

GM Mexico: Tornado **55% of the small-pickup category** at year-end 2010; cumulative >81,000 since 2004 [S16][S17]. **No 2010 unit total** for Tornado, Saveiro, Strada or Courier was fetched from INEGI/AMIA.

## Open gaps / uncertainty

- 2010, 2012, 2013, 2014 lack a fetched full INEGI/AMIA model table for Tornado, Saveiro and Strada. A “top 5” with units for those years **cannot be honestly completed**.
- 2015–2017 have a solid RAM 700 figure and only an upper bound for Saveiro and Tornado.
- Autocosmos 2018 Saveiro (8,198) and VW’s 2018 “Saveiro +18.5%” press line are compatible with a ~6,900 prior-year Saveiro, but 2017 Saveiro units were not in the top 30.
- Wikipedia/carsitaly Strada–Mexico unit series was **not** used as a standalone source; INEGI microdata was not downloaded.
- “Top 5 compact” and “top 5 pickups in Mexico” are different lists; mixing them would put NP300 first every year in the late 2010s [S1][S2].
