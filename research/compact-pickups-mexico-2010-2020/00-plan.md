# Research Plan: Compact Pickups in Mexico, 2010–2020

## Objective
Identify the compact pickups sold in Mexico from 2010 through 2020, rank the top models by year, document engines/horsepower/transmissions, used (resale) prices on the Mexican market, and weigh cheap-but-reliable used-buy value — including advantages, known problems, and a counter-case against “buy the most popular one.” This supports a used-truck purchase decision in Mexico.

## Scope
**In scope:**
- Compact / small pickups (pickups compactas / pequeñas) sold new in Mexico, model years and calendar sales 2010–2020: Chevrolet Tornado, Volkswagen Saveiro, Fiat Strada, RAM 700, Renault Oroch, and any other confirmed compact peers (e.g. Peugeot Hoggar if sold).
- Year-by-year top 5 **within that compact segment**. If fewer than five compact nameplates were on sale, report the actual ranking and state the gap; do not silently fill with full-size trucks.
- Technical specs (engine, horsepower, transmission) for the models that appear in those yearly lists.
- Mexican used/resale price ranges (as observed in this session) for 2010–2020 examples.
- Reliability, common failures, parts availability, and cheap-vs-reliable trade-offs in Mexican use.

**Out of scope:**
- Full-size pickups (Ford F-150/Lobo, Chevrolet Silverado/Cheyenne, RAM 1500, Toyota Tundra).
- Midsize work/lifestyle pickups (Nissan NP300/Frontier, Toyota Hilux, Ford Ranger, Mitsubishi L200, Chevrolet S10/Colorado, VW Amarok, Toyota Tacoma) except as **contrast** for price, reliability, or to explain why official “pickup” rankings are not compact rankings.
- Model years after 2020 except where needed to date a 2020 facelift or current used asking prices.
- Financing products, insurance quotes, or a personal buying recommendation for one VIN.

## Source policy
Prioritize official/primary sources. Reputable secondary sources allowed for corroboration
and context. Exclude unattributed or unverifiable sources. No invented data or citations
anywhere in this project.

Default go-to types: INEGI RAIAVL / AMIA / AMDA sales tables; manufacturer press kits and Mexican price lists; Autocosmos / Motorpasión México / Indicador Automotriz road tests; PROFECO, recall notices (NHTSA only if the same platform was sold in Mexico and the defect is documented as applying); Kavak / Seminuevos / Autocosmos classifieds for **asking** used prices (label as asking prices, not transacted). Wikipedia only as a pointer to primary citations, never as the sole source for a figure.
